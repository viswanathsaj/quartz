A living list of the software and gear I actually rely on day-to-day. Updated as things change.

## Writing

- **Obsidian** — where most thinking happens. Local-first, wikilinks, good enough.
- **iA Writer** — for when I need to focus on a single document without distraction.

## Development

- **Neovim** — after years of VS Code. The learning curve was worth it.
- **WezTerm** — terminal emulator. GPU-rendered, Lua-configurable.
- **Fish shell** — sane defaults, good autocomplete.

## Hardware

- MacBook Pro M3 — daily driver
- Keychron Q1 — tactile switches, heavy enough to stay put
