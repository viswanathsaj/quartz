I used to read to finish. Now I read to think.

The shift happened somewhere around the third time I finished a book and couldn't remember anything from it a week later. The problem wasn't retention — it was that I was treating reading like a task to complete rather than a conversation to have.

## What changed

I started keeping a running note per book. Not a summary — just reactions. Things that surprised me, things I disagreed with, things I wanted to follow up on.

The note doesn't need to be long. Sometimes it's three sentences. But writing it forces me to actually process what I read rather than just scan words.
