Writing about something before you fully understand it is uncomfortable. That discomfort is the point.

When I write a note, I'm forced to find the gaps in my understanding. Vague ideas that felt solid in my head fall apart when I try to put them into sentences. That's useful. The act of writing is the act of thinking.

## Why public

Private notes are fine for capturing things. But publishing — even to a small audience — creates a different kind of accountability. I'm more careful. I check my sources. I try to be precise.

It also creates a feedback loop. Someone who knows more than me can point out what I got wrong.

## The bar is low

This isn't meant to be authoritative. Everything here is provisional. I'll change my mind and update the notes.
